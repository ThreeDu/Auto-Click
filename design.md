# Design — AutoClicker

A locked design system for AutoClicker desktop application.

## Genre
modern-minimal / utilitarian (tactile precision instrument)

## Macrostructure family
- Desktop Utility: Workbench (Hardware Instrument Deck)

## Theme & Palette
- `--color-paper`   #0D0F12 (Obsidian Void)
- `--color-paper-2` #15181E (Technical Deck)
- `--color-paper-3` #1A1E26 (Precision Input Well)
- `--color-border`  #252B36 (1px Hairline Rule)
- `--color-ink`     #F3F4F6 (Crisp Text)
- `--color-ink-2`   #94A3B8 (Technical Muted Labels)
- `--color-ink-dim` #64748B (Dimmed Meta)
- `--color-accent`  #10B981 (Phosphor Emerald Active)
- `--color-alert`   #EF4444 (Signal Red Abort)
- `--color-amber`   #F59E0B (Hardware Hotkey & Recording)
- `--color-cyan`    #0EA5E9 (Digital Readout & Frequency)

## Typography
- Display: Segoe UI, Weight Bold, Normal (Roman headers, no italics, no emojis)
- Body:    Segoe UI, Weight Regular
- Numbers: Consolas, Weight Bold (Tabular numerals for millisecond timing, coordinates, and CPS counters)

## Spacing & Metrics
- Radius: Panel 8px, Controls 5px
- Padding: Card 14-16px

## Microinteractions & Tone
- Pure hardware instrument aesthetic (Raycast / Linear inspired)
- Zero childish emoji decor; clean uppercase micro-kickers
- Monospace digital instrumentation readout for live mouse crosshair and CPS
